# Product Roadmap

The staged implementation plan is maintained in
[`../../PROJECT_PLAN.md`](../../PROJECT_PLAN.md).

## Near-Term Milestones

1. CadQuery geometry proof with STEP and GLB output
2. ReactFlow room/rack/power schematic editor
3. FastAPI generation and artifact delivery
4. Embedded GLB viewer and usability hardening

## Later Capabilities

Cooling topology and thermal calculations, editable multi-room layouts,
2D↔3D selection, cable routing, Postgres/PostGIS persistence, Neo4j topology
queries, capacity simulations, asset lifecycle management, and scalable
background generation are intentionally deferred until the initial vertical
slice is reliable.
